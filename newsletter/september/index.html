Company Perspective

Why We're Building Altitide

Building scalable nucleotide technologies for the next generation of genomics, diagnostics, and biotechnology.

Modern biotechnology needs scale.

Over the past decade, sequencing, automation, and computational biology have advanced rapidly. Yet many biological workflows still depend on sample-preparation processes that are labor-intensive, equipment-dependent, and difficult to scale.

For many applications, the bottleneck is not the ability to analyze biological information. It is the ability to reliably prepare thousands of samples for analysis.

Our Thesis

The next generation of biotechnology infrastructure will require sample-preparation technologies designed for scale from the beginning — not workflows adapted for scale after the fact.

Starting with DNA extraction

Altitide was founded to address this gap by developing high-quality, high-throughput nucleotide technologies.

First Platform

Altitide Bowhead

Bowhead is being developed as a high-throughput platform for high-molecular-weight DNA extraction. The workflow is designed to preserve DNA quality while reducing unnecessary handling and enabling compatibility with manual and automated liquid handling.

Conventional HMW DNA workflows can involve multiple transfers, centrifugation, specialized consumables, and manual manipulation. These requirements can become increasingly difficult to manage as sample numbers increase.

Bowhead is designed around a different principle: simplify the workflow while maintaining the quality required for downstream molecular applications.

Building beyond a single product

Bowhead is the first expression of a broader Altitide strategy: building a platform of nucleotide technologies that make biological workflows easier to scale.

01 · SIMPLIFY

Reduce workflow complexity

Eliminate unnecessary steps and reduce dependence on specialized infrastructure wherever possible.

02 · PRESERVE

Maintain biological quality

Design workflows around the quality requirements of downstream molecular applications.

03 · SCALE

Design for throughput

Build technologies that can move from individual samples toward hundreds or thousands of samples.

04 · AUTOMATE

Enable automation

Develop workflows that can integrate with modern liquid handling and laboratory automation systems.

What we're focused on next

The next stage is turning Bowhead from a working technology into a repeatable commercial product.

  • Continued optimization of Bowhead chemistry and workflow performance.
  • Optimization of the manufacturing and production process.
  • Development of scalable packaging and shipping workflows.
  • Continued testing with early users and beta partners.
  • Preparation for the initial commercial launch of Bowhead.

Our goal is not simply to make another DNA extraction kit. It is to build nucleotide technologies that make biological workflows more scalable.
