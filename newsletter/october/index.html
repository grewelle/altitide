The Science of Longer DNA

Why DNA integrity matters — and how Altitide is designing a simpler, scalable approach to high-molecular-weight DNA extraction.

Altitide Science Update   •   Bowhead Technology

Better genomics begins with better DNA.

Long-read sequencing can reveal complex genomic regions, structural variation, and repetitive sequences. But these technologies depend on starting material that remains long, intact, and representative of the original biological sample.

01 / Why length matters

DNA integrity shapes the information researchers can access.

During extraction, naturally long DNA molecules can become fragmented through mechanical stress, chemical exposure, and excessive sample handling. The DNA may remain usable for some applications, but shorter fragments can limit the information available to long-read sequencing.

Longer fragments can help researchers resolve complex genomic regions, characterize structural variation, span repetitive sequences, and improve genome assembly.

DNA length and integrity are important dimensions of sample quality.
02 / The extraction challenge

Recover DNA while preserving its structure.

Traditional workflows can involve multiple processing steps, transfers, mixing operations, and specialized equipment. Each additional handling step can create opportunities for DNA loss or fragmentation.

A successful high-molecular-weight workflow must balance recovery, purity, integrity, and reproducibility. High concentration alone does not necessarily indicate intact DNA.

Workflow design is part of DNA quality.
03 / Altitide's approach

Designed around DNA integrity.

Altitide is developing Bowhead, a high-throughput HMW DNA extraction technology built around silica-bead chemistry and a streamlined pipette-based workflow.

The design emphasizes controlled fluid handling and a simplified extraction architecture, with the goal of reducing unnecessary manipulation while supporting compatibility with liquid-handling automation.

Bowhead · Silica-bead HMW DNA extraction concept
Minimize handlingReduce unnecessary transfers and processing steps that can contribute to sample loss or mechanical stress.
Control fluid handlingUse wide-bore pipette tips and controlled mixing to support the handling of long DNA molecules.
Build for scaleDesign for pipette-based workflows and potential liquid-handler automation, from individual samples to larger batches.
04 / Designed for throughput

From individual samples to automated workflows.

Bowhead is being developed with the goal of scaling sample preparation without proportionally increasing equipment requirements or operational complexity.

Potential users include academic sequencing cores, genomics laboratories, biotechnology companies, and research organizations preparing samples for long-read sequencing.

A workflow designed with liquid-handling automation in mind.
05 / What we measure

Performance is more than yield.

Altitide's development program evaluates multiple dimensions of extraction performance to build a more complete picture of sample quality and downstream suitability.
