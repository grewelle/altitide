Four beta partners. One major step toward scalable DNA preparation.

Altitide is moving from internal development toward real-world validation with four organizations spanning sequencing, academic research, diagnostics, and natural-history research.

The takeaway: Our beta program is designed to test whether a simpler, automation-friendly approach to high-molecular-weight DNA extraction can perform reliably across diverse sample types and laboratory environments.

Our four beta testing partners

The strength of this group is its diversity. We are putting the technology in the hands of teams with different applications and operating requirements.

1 → 1,000s
Our product vision: a high-molecular-weight DNA workflow that can scale from individual samples to thousands of samples using standard liquid-handling infrastructure.

Why the beta program matters

Altitide is developing a different approach to DNA purification: minimize physical handling of DNA while eliminating unnecessary equipment and consumables. The goal is a workflow that can be performed with a pipette or liquid handler, without requiring centrifuges, specialized plates, or mechanical mixers.

The beta program will help us quantify performance, identify edge cases, refine protocols, and determine where the platform delivers the greatest customer value. Feedback from independent users will also shape the product before broader commercial launch.

What comes next

Over the coming months, we expect to focus on beta validation, protocol optimization, and preparation for commercial launch. We will share results as data become available, including performance benchmarks and lessons learned from partner workflows.

We believe the opportunity is larger than a single extraction kit. A simpler, scalable sample-preparation layer could become valuable infrastructure for sequencing, diagnostics, research, and other applications where high-quality DNA is the starting point.
